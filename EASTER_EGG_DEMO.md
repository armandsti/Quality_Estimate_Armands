# 📊 **STATISTICS PANEL**

## 🎯 **What It Does**

The statistics panel shows app-wide statistics in a clean, professional way at the top-right corner of the History page.

## 🎮 **How to View It**

1. **Go to the History page** of your app
2. **Look at the top-right corner** - you'll see the statistics panel
3. **The panel is always visible** and shows real-time data
4. **No interaction required** - statistics are displayed immediately

## 📊 **Statistics Shown**

The easter egg displays **app-wide statistics**:

- **Total Segments**: Total QA errors across all reports
- **✅ Confirmed**: Total segments marked as accepted
- **❌ Rejected**: Total segments marked as rejected  
- **✏️ Edited**: Total segments with custom suggestions
- **📊 Total Reports**: Number of analysis reports in history

## 🎨 **Visual Design**

- **Always visible**: Statistics panel is permanently displayed
- **Top-right positioning**: Clean, professional placement
- **Glassmorphism effect**: Semi-transparent with backdrop blur
- **Color-coded stats**: Green for confirmed, red for rejected, blue for edited
- **Monospace numbers**: Clean, readable statistics
- **History page only**: Only appears on the History page
